Design

Beautiful. And quiet about it.

Regards is made to be looked at all day: clean type, room to breathe, and nothing that blinks for your attention. It is the same on Windows, macOS and Linux.

LightDark

Light and dark

Two themes, both designed with care. In the dark one, your messages get a dark page too.

Your layout

The message beside the list, or in its place. The sidebar wide, or folded to icons.

Every key is yours

Do everything from the keyboard, and change any shortcut you like.

In your language

In English and in French, following your system.

The inbox

Calm when you're in it. Handled when you're not.

Sorted for you

See your inbox by priority, by category or by date. People come first.

New senders ask first

The first message from an unknown address waits for you to accept or block it.

Done, pin, set aside

Clear what's finished, keep what matters on top, and park the rest.

Snooze and reminders

Hide a message until Monday, or be reminded if nobody answers.

Send later, undo send

Schedule a message, or take it back in the seconds after you press Send.

Undo everywhere

Every action, yours or your agent's, can be undone. Press ? to see every key.

Search everything

Find mail stored on your device and mail still on the server, in one search.

All your accounts

Several addresses in one window, each with its own folders and signatures.

Open source

Built in the open. Yours to read, build and change.

A mail app holds your whole life, so you should be able to check what it does. Every line of Regards is public: the app, the mail engine and the agent interface. Nothing is closed, and nothing phones home.
