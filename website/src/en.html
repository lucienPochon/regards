The inbox

Calm when you're in it. Handled when you're not.

Sorted for you

See your inbox by priority, by category or by date. People come first.

New senders ask first

The first message from an unknown address waits for you to accept or block it.

Done, pin, set aside

Clear what's finished, keep what matters on top, and park the rest.

Snooze and reminders

Hide a message until Monday, or be reminded if nobody answers.

Send later, undo send

Schedule a message, or take it back in the seconds after you press Send.

Undo everywhere

Search everything

Find mail stored on your device and mail still on the server, in one search.

All your accounts

Free

No price. No catch.

Other mail apps charge every month for AI. Regards is free, and works with the agent you already have.

  • Every feature
  • All your addresses
  • Agent access
  • No ads, ever
  • No account with us
  • No trial that ends

One optional paid service is planned for version 1.1: sending while your computer is off. The app itself stays free.

Open source

Read every line.

A mail app holds your whole life. You should be able to check what it does, so nothing in Regards is closed.

  • No Regards servers. The app talks only to your mail provider.
  • No telemetry and no analytics.
  • Passwords stay in your system's credential store.
  • Remote images are blocked until you allow them.
